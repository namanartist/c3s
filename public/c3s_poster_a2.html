1. Abstract

C3S (Campus Safety and Security System) is an integrated, browser-native emergency dispatch and indoor/outdoor navigational platform engineered specifically for the Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS) campus.

Students trigger an emergency alert via a 2-second hold SOS button, instantly transmitting calibrated GPS/floor-node coordinates, live camera frames, and tactical location to campus quick-response teams. The platform consolidates multi-floor vector indoor navigation, real-time edge computer vision aggression classification, cryptographic HMAC gate check-in, and tamper-evident audit logging into five synchronized role-based dashboards with full offline fallback capability.

2. Problem & Objectives

Campus Reality
  • Delayed Response: Phone-call reporting takes 8–9 minutes, fraught with vague landmark descriptions.
  • Siloed Systems: CCTV feeds, physical paper gate registers, and security walkie-talkies operate in disconnected silos.
  • Indoor Blind Spots: External GPS services fail inside multi-floor academic blocks (e.g. AI Block, Main Building).
Key Objectives
  • Sub-2-Minute Interception: Dispatch the nearest active guard with precise floor and room coordinates.
  • Unified Single-Pane Platform: Seamlessly bind navigation, AI surveillance, gate passes, and audit trails.
  • Edge Privacy & Zero Latency: Run computer vision client-side without costly cloud streaming or external GPU servers.

5. Key Innovation: Migrating from YOLO11 to OpenCV

In initial prototype iterations, our team evaluated cloud-based YOLO11 deep neural networks for incident detection. However, practical campus deployment revealed crippling bottlenecks in bandwidth cost, network jitter, and privacy compliance. We re-engineered the perception layer into a sub-2ms client-side OpenCV optical flow & kinetic jerk vision engine.

✖ Previous: Cloud YOLO11
  • Heavy Latency: 450ms–1.2s cloud inferencing + frame video streaming overhead.
  • Expensive Hardware: Dedicated server GPUs required ($250+/month per 4 camera streams).
  • Bandwidth Bottleneck: 12 continuous HD streams saturated campus wireless links.
  • Privacy Hazard: Student video streams continuously sent outside local network.
✔ Deployed: Edge OpenCV Engine
  • Real-time Sub-2ms: Zero network transit; processed directly in browser canvas/Wasm.
  • Zero GPU Cloud Cost: Runs on existing commodity guard laptops and tablets.
  • Minimal Bandwidth: Only lightweight event metadata is signaled upon threshold breach.
  • 100% Data Privacy: Video buffers never leave memory; GDPR & DPDP compliant.

7. Mathematical Modeling

1. Non-Blocking Dijkstra Pathfinding:
$$d(v) = \min_{u \in \text{adj}(v)} \{ d(u) + w(u, v) \}$$ Yields to browser animation loop via requestAnimationFrame every 50 iterations to prevent UI freezing across 641 graph nodes.
2. Emergency Response Time Model:
$$T = t_{\text{detect}} + t_{\text{alert}} + \frac{s}{v_{\text{responder}}}$$ Reduces total latency $T$ from 537s (phone call) to 98s (empirical drill) by minimizing $t_{\text{alert}} \approx 1.2\text{s}$.
3. Crowd Density & Kinetic Jerk:
$$\rho = \frac{N}{A} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{J} = \frac{d^2 \mathbf{v}}{dt^2} = \frac{d^3 \mathbf{x}}{dt^3}$$ Sudden vector acceleration spikes ($|\mathbf{J}| > \theta_{\text{fight}}$) trigger instant edge alerts without heavy neural classifiers.
4. Dispatch Arrival (Poisson Distribution):
$$P(k \text{ incidents in time } t) = \frac{(\lambda t)^k e^{-\lambda t}}{k!}$$ Guarantees queue convergence under peak rush hour campus incident loads.

9. Future Scope: Radar 3D Indoor Positioning

While vector SVG maps and Wi-Fi anchors provide 2D campus navigation, our roadmap introduces millimeter-wave (mmWave) Radar Sensing for true 3D indoor coordinate positioning.

  • Altitude & Multi-floor Discrimination ($z$-axis): Radar point clouds accurately discern whether an SOS originates from Ground, 1st, or 2nd floor, eliminating vertical ambiguities.
  • Through-Wall & Obscuration Sensing: Functions flawlessly through dense smoke, fire, and unlit corridors where optical CCTV is blinded.
  • Augmented Reality (AR) Wayfinding: Overlaying 3D floating navigational vectors directly on student phone camera views.
